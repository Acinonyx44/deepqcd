1Protocol

The network, its training and the transient metrics are the paper's and the authors' (LSTM(16) → Dense(10) → Dense(1), cross-entropy, 20 epochs; original_qcd.py). Five things change against the earlier studies, to answer the open points: thresholds chosen on the test data, one seed, no intervals, no stronger baseline, and windows instead of whole histories.

  • Three periods. Training before p₀, calibration [p₀, p₁), test [p₁, p₂). Every threshold is chosen on the calibration period (the paper's Eq. 11 there) and applied unchanged to the test period, which reports the realized PFA next to PD. Eq. 11 on the test streams themselves is kept as the oracle curve.
  • Five seeds and their ensemble. The detector is the mean of five DeepQCD outputs, the version one would deploy.
  • Bootstrap intervals. 1,000 resamples of the event streams and of the normal windows give 95% intervals for PD and PFA, and for the paired difference in PD between DeepQCD and gradient boosting.
  • Gradient boosting (LightGBM on the current step, the five before it, and the mean and maximum of each feature over two recent windows) joins the logistic model, the window-limited CUSUM and the rules of the trade.
  • Streaming. Every account or card is scored at every transaction of the calibration and test periods, from its last PRE + 1 transactions, as a bank would run the alarm. The threshold gives a target number of false alert-days per 1,000 active entity-days in the calibration period.
